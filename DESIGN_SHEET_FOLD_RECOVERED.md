# DESIGN SHEET — FOLD (RECOVERED): prior + attention-pool + content-confidence residual
Status: LOCKED by author 2026-07-10 ("sounds good, lock in, write down and run"). This RECOVERS the
June attention-pool design + Bayesian-shrinkage no-harm property, ports them to the RecVAE-d512 world,
and keeps fold-v3's valid two-channel contribution. NOT a reinvention. Supersedes v4(de-OOD)/v5(CNP).

## 0. Why this exists (the regression it undoes)
The June belief encoder was a SIMPLE ATTENTION-POOL (count-normalized) with residual-on-native and
Bayesian-shrinkage no-harm. The RecVAE rebuild (i25/v3, Jul 7) silently swapped it for a SUM-pool
residual with a raw log1p(ntok) input "for v1 speed" — introducing the cold-intercept bug + the
cardinality confound + the Q1 drop. Set-transformer/MHSA was rejected 3x for elicitation (overfits
short sequences) — do NOT reintroduce it.

## 1. Architecture (stated as REQUIREMENTS; the gates enforce them)
z = prior + w·delta, over the FROZEN RecVAE-d512 decoder/scorer.
- PRIOR (the intercept): native_z = the FIXED population/cold prior (RecVAE's empty-set encoding),
  INDEPENDENT of the revealed items. Do NOT jump it to a single-item RecVAE encoding (that caused the
  Q1 drop). This makes cold ≡ prior, a true shared intercept.
- DELTA: ATTENTION-POOL over answer tokens (softmax weights sum to 1 → count-normalized), then a
  small residual MLP. NOT sum-pool. NOT set-transformer/MHSA (rejected 3x).
- CONFIDENCE GATE w: driven by CONTENT, not count — per-token FIDELITY (real rating > EASE > vague)
  and multi-token COHERENCE (agreeing answers pull harder; noisy/contradictory ones partly cancel).
  w MUST be 0 when there are no tokens (empty → prior exactly) and MUST NOT take ntok as an input.
  This restores Bayesian shrinkage: weak evidence → stay near prior (no Q1 drop); consistent evidence
  → confident move; noise → down-weighted (no full-profile degradation).
- NO hand-crafted SURPRISE. Feed the RAW ingredients per token: log(n_E), log(V), log(p_E) + the
  512-d entity embedding; let the network learn the combination (it can recover surprise or better).
- KEEP: two-channel tokens (implicit consumption + explicit value), fidelity/knowledge-level feature,
  concepts/attributes/entities as learned channels.

## 2. Answers — DATA-SIDE ONLY (non-circular, author-confirmed)
Item = real centered rating (fid=data); concept/attr/entity = real-rating aggregate over revealed
members; unrated-item base = EASE. cos(z*, q) / any recommender-geometry answer appears NOWHERE in
training or evaluation (the geometric answer was circular; it is OUT). Refusals = low-reliability
tokens (weak "not-in-world" signal), not dropped.

## 3. Training
Reconstruct held-out LIKED items from masked partial reveals, through the FROZEN RecVAE decoder
(multinomial log-likelihood, known items masked). IPS / inverse-popularity weighting (June recipe).
- CURRICULUM (must cover the whole deployment spectrum): reveals of ARBITRARY length (1..24), varying
  QUALITY and REFUSAL rate, generated by a MIXTURE of realistic blind selection strategies
  (random/popularity/entropy/blind-EIG/on-profile-as-one-flavor/off-profile/mixed-schedule) — NOT the
  privileged on_profile=0.7 (OOD), NOT fat tails (v4 collapse). Realistic tail weights (~0.15-0.20).
  PLUS ~30% clean FULL profiles (so it stays strong on full profiles). Hold out ONE strategy for the
  generalization gate.
- Frozen decoder, deterministic seeds, all checkpoints, best-on-disjoint-val.

## 4. TRAIN / VAL / TEST SPLIT (author-flagged — exact, disjoint, no leak)
- The 173 LLM-judged / 300 study users: QUARANTINED. NEVER in fold train/val/test. Not touched.
- Fold cohorts from the ML-25M population trU users (study IDs excluded by construction):

- Every gate/number reported on TEST users the fold never trained or validated on.

## 5. Gate suite (FULL recovered — Paper A + fold + new; printed before results, on TEST)
NEW (this session):
- G-intercept: fold([]) == native_z to tolerance; cold NDCG == native cold NDCG (fold-independent).
- G-falsify-count: DUPLICATE test flat (fold identical answers ×2/×3 → NDCG unchanged) AND
  uninformative-padding curve flat. (The honesty test: gains are information, not cardinality.)
- G-generalize: held-out (unseen) selection strategy within tolerance of seen strategies.
- G-order: permutation/shuffle invariance (V2-A failed this; must hold).
RECOVERED fold gates:
- G-noharm: revealing a real answer never hurts (Bayesian shrinkage).
- G-noQ1drop: NO massive drop after Q1; per-turn NDCG non-decreasing (max step ≥ −0.003).
- G-clean: full-profile fold ≥ native − 0.02.
- G-caplength: full-profile skill NOT bought at elicitation cost (elicitation ≥ prior baseline).
- G-canaries: 1 answer per channel×token-type from cold HELPS (item/concept/attr/entity × impl/expl).
- G-GoT + G-prolific: consumption-as-taste (watched-all-and-hated pulled toward region; selective >
  prolific).
- G-implicit-ablation: zeroing implicit tokens DROPS NDCG (channel load-bearing).
- G-firewall: assert cos(z*, q) / recommender-geometry answers absent from train+eval (circularity).
