# External Literature INDEX — CASPER PhD

All 90 entries in `new_chapters/references.bib`, sorted by topic-tag then year.
Takeaways are drawn ONLY from the project's own summary/deep-research sources (see `README.md`);
papers not discussed anywhere carry `(citation only — not yet summarized)`. Bib-keys are preserved
so every claim is traceable. `relevant-to` = which CASPER paper (A/B/C/D/E) the finding feeds; `—` = none/background.

## Tag legend
- **cont-RL** — continuous-action / deep RL methods (DDPG, DQN, Wolpertinger, TD3, SAC, ECoC)
- **CRS-classic** — pre-LLM conversational recommender systems and adaptive dialogue strategies
- **CRS-LLM** — LLM-based conversational recommenders (2022–2025)
- **CRS-RL** — RL / graph policy learning for CRS (EAR, SCPR, UNICORN, PPDPP, RSO, ConTS)
- **CRS-survey** — surveys / reviews of the CRS field
- **elicit-AL** — rating elicitation, active learning, cold-start interview trees
- **elicit-belief** — Bayesian belief / VoI / info-gain preference elicitation
- **embed-infra** — embedding & retrieval infrastructure (SBERT, FAISS)
- **rec-model** — recommender models & evaluation methodology (MF, VAE, FM, top-N eval)
- **bot-play** — self-supervised bot-play / multi-agent dialogue RL
- **behav** — behavioral / choice psychology
- **misc** — datasets, template, non-research

## Addendum — BANDITS / RL / attribute-asking CRS: what the family prices, and which policies port (added 2026-08-05; see `findings/bandit_rl_crs_baselines.md`)

> Paper B baseline battery, family = interactive-CF bandits / RL / conversational RS. For every method: (a) what its reward/metric really prices, (b) does it need a known target item or an omniscient simulator, (c) does it report ANY post-interview held-out ranking, (d) is its POLICY portable to our contract even though its NUMBERS are not. Full analysis + per-method table + effort estimates → `findings/bandit_rl_crs_baselines.md`.
>
> **THE CORRECTION: there is an entire family we had missed — CONVERSATIONAL BANDITS (key-term bandits).** ConUCB (WWW'20) → ConLinUCB (AAAI'23) → Hier-UCB (2022) → **CLiSK/CLiME (KDD'25, on ML-25M with genres as key-terms)** → FedConPE (2024). They ask about a **key-term (attribute/genre/tag) OR an item** in one algorithm, model how key-term feedback moves the item-level estimate, carry regret bounds, and have **NO known target item and no omniscient simulator** — reward is replayed real feedback. This is our item-vs-concept question channel, formalised. **NOMINATION: ConLinUCB-MCR (argmax_c alpha_t * ||d_c||_{M_t^-1}) over our frozen tower's Gaussian posterior with tag-genome concepts as key-terms — ~4-8 agent-hours on top of the `bandit_selectors.py` rig we already ship, and the only published adaptive method whose action space is ours and whose protocol passes the behavioural firewall as-is.**
>
> **CONTRACT AUDIT CONCLUSION: outside the rating-elicitation family, EXACTLY ONE method in the bandit/RL/CRS literature reports a post-interview held-out ranking metric over a full catalogue — GREEDY SLIM (arXiv:2406.06061, CoRR-only, which the Aug-4 sweep missed): unfiltered ML-25M (162,541u/59,047i), cold user = a zero row, answers copied from REAL held-out ratings, no item sampling, NDCG@10 at k in {5,10,15,20}, AND a long-tail split defined exactly as ours (Cremonesi 33%-of-ratings head). Its adaptive foil is Christakopoulou 2016 ("QBandit"), whose NDCG DECREASES past k~10 on BOTH axes while the static questionnaire keeps climbing — an independent external replication of our bank-quality effect and a published precedent for the shape of Act 1. A ~93-query arXiv+DBLP sweep found nothing else; the cleanest supporting cite is Gupta/Oosterhuis/de Rijke own admission that "no publicly available recommendation dataset that has preference elicitation interactions" exists [V]. Everything else:** Bandits score the probes themselves (cumulative precision/regret over the interaction); the CRS line scores hitting a known target (Success-Rate@turn / Average-Turns). **Numbers not orderable; several policies portable — and a policy whose numbers are unusable is still a MUST-INCLUDE once re-scored under our contract.** Because none can be number-matched, the HARD-RULE-2 gate for a ported policy is: reproduce the *selection rule* exactly, beat random+popularity on our metric, and label the row "policy ported, protocol replaced".
>
> **Three new self-reported disqualification quotes (stronger than anything we had):** PTS (NeurIPS'15) restricts recommendations "to items rated by this user in the dataset" [V]; COFIBA (SIGIR'16) "we simulated random choices by the system" (candidate sets of 10-20 items) [V]; and the CRS candidate pool is not merely shrinking but **seeded by an attribute of the target** — MCMIPL/DAHCR/CoCHPL/VPMCR all initialise V_cand from p_0 in P_v [V x4], **and none of them reports the resulting pool size** (MCMIPL and VPMCR report zero candidate-set statistics [V]). **Fourth, and the cleanest novelty claim: ICF's own metric "theta_hit = 1 if the rating is no less than 4, and 0 otherwise" [V] cannot distinguish "never saw it" from "disliked it" — no method in this lineage can price a burned question.**
>
> **Also settled: MINICORN (arXiv:2206.14468) uses NO RL** — MC-dropout epistemic uncertainty + midpoint proximity, harmonic mean, ask the max-uncertainty attribute — and reports **+67.40% SR / +13.39% AT over SCPR on LastFM** [V]. Cite whenever asked why we did not build an RL policy. **The benchmarks are also saturated** (CoCHPL 0.991 SR@15 on LastFM at 3.75 turns; DAHCR 0.992 on Yelp [V]).
>
> **⚠ DO-NOT-CITE / verification debts (full list in the findings file):** Filippi's exact confidence radius (a plausible OFUL-style formula was **confabulated** and rejected); **Pseudo Dyna-Q (WSDM'20) is not on arXiv and its DOI 403s — cite no DOI**; **CT-CRS arXiv:2508.13209 is WITHDRAWN by its authors yet still surfaces in search results — never cite**; IGCF's datasets/numbers/venue; factorUCB's alleged ranking metrics; Q&R's offline protocol; RLT4Rec's datasets/metrics; **the identity of ICF Table 2's `AL` and `Interview` baselines — chase it, ICF beats `Interview` by only 1.6%**.
