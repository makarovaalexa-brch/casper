# ECIR style corpus — index

Purpose: a small corpus of recent ECIR full papers (Springer LNCS, *Advances in Information Retrieval*) used only to calibrate the writing style of the CASPER Paper B submission (ECIR 2027). All files are public arXiv preprints downloaded with `curl` on 2026-09-08. Venue/track status was confirmed from the arXiv "Comments" field of each record (quoted below). Page counts are of the arXiv PDF (LNCS layout, references included).
