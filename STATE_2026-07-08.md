## The two-regime finding (the honest headline)
- **v1 (scarcity regime):** answerable items are rare and user-specific → the adaptive problem is
  DISCOVERY ("which items can this user answer?"). The prize for solving it is measured: +0.060
  within the deployable policy class, +0.21 clairvoyant. Our blind discoverers (co-rating affinity,
  pmodel-with-online-taste) both fail: hit rate rises 14%→18% but lands on low-value items; the
  online taste estimate DEGRADES over the interview (0.86→0.46 — refusal-subtractions swamp 4
  answers of evidence). The estimator is broken, not the idea.
- **v2 (abundance regime):** with measured answerability, ~everything popular is answerable (97%
  hit) → the discovery problem VANISHES (the true-table ceiling itself adds nothing) → the game
  shifts to VALUE/FIDELITY: unrated items answer with noisy inferred ratings, and folding many
  vague answers DILUTES the few vivid ones (s3's own score drops 0.279→0.238 while its hit rate
  septuples — more answers, worse belief!). The clairvoyant prize explodes (+0.37) because knowing
  WHICH answers are vivid is now worth everything.
- **Together:** the answerability regime sets WHICH problem adaptivity must solve — finding
  answerable questions (scarcity) or finding high-fidelity ones (abundance). No prior work states
  this; it is the adaptivity-side twin of the fidelity boundary, and both regimes' prizes are
  measured with privileged ceilings. This slots straight into the channel-map flagship.

## What tonight's failures teach (each diagnosed, none mysterious)
1. Info-greedy selection is anti-correlated with value under a strong fold (twice reproduced).
2. The online knowledge estimator (ĝ) must be redesigned: refusals currently subtract too
   aggressively; with 4 answers/24 the posterior moves AWAY from truth.
3. In the abundance regime the fold needs FIDELITY AWARENESS: confidence-weighted answer tokens
   (the answerer can realistically report "seen it, loved it" vs "think I'd like it"), and the
   agent should probe for VIVID memories, not just answerable items. This is mechanical and
   testable, and it is the v2 counterpart of the v1 estimator fix.
4. (Standing from a4:) the pmodel ranks but its LEVEL is arena-relative — always recalibrate the
   threshold to the arena's base rate before thresholding.
